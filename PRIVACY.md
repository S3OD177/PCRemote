# Privacy Policy for PC Remote

Effective date: October 8, 2026

PC Remote does not collect, sell, share, or transmit personal data to the app
developer or to third-party analytics or advertising services.

The app communicates directly with a computer selected by the user on the same
local network. It stores connection details and a pairing secret locally on the
user's device. The pairing secret is stored using the operating system's secure
credential storage. Camera access is used only to scan a pairing QR code; images
and video are not recorded, retained, or uploaded.

Local network access is required to discover and control the paired computer.
The Windows companion stores its pairing state locally on that computer. Users
can reset the pairing secret in the companion to revoke all paired devices.

If the app's data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy
answers must be updated before a new version is released.

For privacy questions, open an issue in this repository. Before App Store
submission, the publisher should replace this sentence with a monitored support
email address or support URL.
