## Description

**English:**
```
PC Remote turns your iPhone into a remote control for your Windows computer.
From the comfort of your couch or bed, you can:

• Shut down your PC completely
• Put it to sleep — and wake it again
• Lock the screen behind your password

How it works
1. Install the free PC Remote helper on your Windows PC (link below).
2. Open the app and scan the pairing code the helper shows.
3. The buttons light up. Control your PC any time you're on the same Wi-Fi.

Private and secure by design
• No account, no sign-up, no ads, no analytics.
• Nothing leaves your home network — the app talks only to your own PC.
• Pairing shares a secret key by QR code only; every command is signed, so no
  other app or website can control your computer.

Requirements
• A Windows PC running the free PC Remote helper.
• Your iPhone and PC on the same Wi-Fi network.
• Wake PC requires Wake-on-LAN support on your computer.
```

## ⚠️ App Review notes (paste into "Notes" in the Submit screen)

This is the most important part for approval. The app needs a Windows PC
running a companion helper, which the reviewer does not have. Tell them clearly,
and attach a short screen recording.

**Suggested text:**
```
PC Remote is a companion app for a free Windows helper that the user installs on
their own PC. The app sends local-network commands (shutdown, sleep, lock, wake)
to that PC. It connects only over the local network to a user-owned computer
(Guideline 4.2.7 compliant). There is no login and we collect no data.

Because reviewing requires a paired Windows PC on the same network, we have
attached a demonstration video showing: installing the helper, pairing by QR
code, and sending each command.

The Local Network permission is required to reach the user's PC. The Camera
permission is used once, only to scan the pairing QR code. The multicast
entitlement is used only to send a Wake-on-LAN broadcast packet to wake the
user's own PC.

Helper download for testing (optional): <your link>
```

**Attach a demo video** (App Review → App Review Information → add a video, or
link one). Record your iPhone screen pairing + pressing each button, with the PC
visible. 30–60 seconds is enough. This single step prevents most rejections for
companion apps.
```
